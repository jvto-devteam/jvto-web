# Product

## Register
product

## Users
International travelers (primarily European, Australian, American) who have decided to do a volcano tour in East Java and are now in the decision-to-book workflow. Their primary tasks: compare package options, verify the operator's credentials, select dates and pax count, complete checkout (Xendit deposit), and track their booking post-payment. They arrive skeptical — is this operator real and capable? — and leave that skepticism behind through documented evidence, not marketing copy.

## Product Purpose
JVTO sells operational certainty for East Java volcano tours (Bromo, Ijen, Tumpak Sewu). Licensed Indonesian operator (PT Java Volcano Rendezvous, NIB 1102230032918), led by an active Tourist Police officer. The product converts skeptical researchers into confirmed bookings via a self-service checkout flow (Xendit), then supports them through a customer dashboard (my-booking) up to and through the trip. Every screen serves one of three jobs: build enough trust to proceed, complete a transaction step, or manage an existing booking.

## Brand Personality
Authoritative. Transparent. Precise.

## Anti-references
- Backpacker fun sites (Viator, GetYourGuide generic listings) — too casual, undermines operational authority
- Generic SaaS UI kits — wrong domain, wrong emotional register
- Generic travel blogs — no operational depth, no task orientation
- Luxury resort aesthetics — wrong price-point and wrong audience expectation
- Sterile government portals — forensic but cold; trust without humanity
- Badge-heavy audit boards — trust theater, not trust evidence

## Design Principles
1. Verifiability over vague credibility — every trust claim links to a verifiable source
2. Authority without theatrics — operational confidence, not performative safety-washing
3. Support as infrastructure — contact and help are structural, not afterthoughts
4. Structured truth beats decorative trust — schema-marked facts over styled social proof
5. Consistency matters more than decoration — predictable patterns build more confidence than one-off visual moments

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. No additional known requirements.
