Product Overview

Java Volcano Tour Operator (JVTO) is a registered Indonesian private tour company — legal entity PT Java Volcano Rendezvous, headquartered in Bondowoso, East Java. Founded in 2016 by Agung Sambuko (Mr. Sam), an active Indonesian Tourist Police officer (Ditpamobvit), JVTO delivers all-inclusive private volcano itineraries to Mount Bromo, Ijen Crater, Tumpak Sewu, Madakaripura, and Papuma Beach. Every booking is private — no shared group buses, no strangers in your vehicle. The website serves two audiences simultaneously: travelers ready to book, and skeptical first-time visitors who need to verify legitimacy before committing.

Core Brand Position
Operational Certainty
In a landscape where volcanic nature is unpredictable, JVTO sells what it can actually control: disciplined logistics, documented credentials, and consistent local execution — not marketing promises about the mountain.
Private-Only Operator
Your Group. Your Vehicle. Your Pace.
JVTO does not operate join-group or open bus tours. Every package is a private departure — solo travelers, couples, families, and corporate groups each get a dedicated vehicle and crew assigned exclusively to them.
All-Inclusive Pricing
One Price. No Surprise Fees.
Every JVTO package covers private transport, accommodation, entrance permits, licensed guides, gas masks (Ijen), mandatory health screening (Ijen), meals as stated, mineral water, and a JVTO travel T-shirt. Tiered group pricing means larger groups pay less per person.

Booking Confirmation Rules
A booking is only confirmed when ALL THREE of the following conditions are met simultaneously:
1. Guest selects a specific official JVTO package (by route/slug, e.g. /tours/from-surabaya/bromo-madakaripura-ijen-3d2n)
2. Required payment is successfully processed: 20% deposit via approved method (or 100% full payment if Day 1 is within 14 days)
3. JVTO issues the Official E-Voucher / Invoice (PDF) confirming booking details, pickup info, and emergency contacts
Enquiries, WhatsApp messages, and quotations are NOT binding until confirmed under the above rule. Display this clearly on booking pages.
