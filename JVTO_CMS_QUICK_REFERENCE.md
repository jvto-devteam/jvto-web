## What to Avoid

### ❌ Do NOT
- Turn CMS into operations platform yet (booking, logistics, finance)
- Make blog the center of the system (it's an output layer)
- Allow unrestricted route/slug editing
- Merge public crew profiles with internal ops
- Generate content without SSOT constraints
- Skip the trust architecture (Phase 2) - it's JVTO's main differentiator

### ✅ DO
- Keep SSOT as the source of truth
- Implement guardrails on routes & claims
- Separate concerns (public, internal, derivative)
- Maintain narrative consistency
- Version control proof/credentials
- Document everything as JVTO-native, not generic
