Technical SEO
C1 — Homepage @graph serialised as numeric-keyed object
Live crawl shows the homepage JSON-LD @graph array contains a single object with key "0": {…} instead of a flat array of nodes. Google's parser and AI answer engines treat this as an invalid graph — the TravelAgency and Organization entities are invisible to structured data processors. This alone suppresses knowledge panel eligibility and answer engine citation.
Fix: ensure builders.ts outputs @graph: [ node1, node2 ] — not @graph: [ {"0": node1} ]. Validate with Google Rich Results Test after deploy.
C2 — Canonical tag missing on 28 of 46 live pages
Only 4 URLs returned a canonical tag in the live crawl: the homepage, /destinations, and two policy pages. Every tour page (16 URLs), hub pages (/tours, /tours/from-surabaya, /tours/from-bali), /why-jvto, /verify-jvto, /travel-guide, and /policy are missing canonicals. Without canonical signals, Google may consolidate or discount crawl priority; AI scrapers may index duplicates.
Fix: add <link rel="canonical" href="https://javavolcano-touroperator.com{route}" /> via the Next.js 14 metadata.alternates.canonical field on every page. The SSOT route field is the canonical path.
C3 — Destination pages (Ijen, Bromo, etc.) return HTTP 404
All 5 destination detail pages listed in the sitemap (/destinations/ijen-crater, /destinations/mount-bromo, /destinations/tumpak-sewu-waterfall, /destinations/madakaripura-waterfall, /destinations/papuma-beach) return 404. These are in the sitemap and SSOT, meaning Google is crawling and indexing 404 errors for your highest-value destination keywords — actively damaging rankings for "Ijen Crater tour", "Bromo sunrise tour" etc.
Priority 1: deploy destination detail pages immediately. These are the pages most likely to earn AI-engine citations and travel blog backlinks. Even a minimal version with SSOT data is better than 404.
C4 — H1 missing on 5 key pages including /tours, /tours/from-surabaya, /tours/from-bali, /verify-jvto
These hub and trust pages have no <h1> in the DOM. H1 is the single most important on-page signal for AI answer engines (which extract it as the "topic" of a page). Google SGE and Perplexity use H1 as primary topical anchor when constructing citations.
Add H1 matching the SSOT h1 field for each page. Example: /tours → "Private East Java Tours: Surabaya & Bali Departures"; /verify-jvto → "Verify JVTO: Legal Documents & Proof of Operations".
H1 — Meta descriptions exceed 160 characters on all 16 tour pages
Live crawl shows meta descriptions ranging from 241–288 characters on tour pages. Google truncates at ~155–160 characters in SERPs; AI answer engines use the meta description as a "confidence signal" for relevance — truncated or stuffed descriptions reduce citation probability. The SSOT descriptions are also over-length.
Rewrite meta descriptions to 140–155 characters. Lead with the unique differentiator (e.g. "Tourist Police-led private tour…") not with generic booking text. Update SSOT first, then propagate.
H2 — Title tags on /tours, /why-jvto, /policy, /destinations have duplicate brand suffix "| JVTO Tours" appended to "| JVTO"
Several pages show "… | JVTO | JVTO Tours" — double-brand suffix. This wastes the ~60-char title budget on redundant text and looks unprofessional in SERPs. SSOT title tags do not include this duplication — it is being injected by the legacy template layer.
Remove the legacy "| JVTO Tours" suffix from the global layout. The SSOT title_tag field already ends with the correct brand. Use the SSOT value verbatim.

Schema gap: no TouristAttraction or Place entity for Ijen, Bromo, Tumpak Sewu
Destination detail pages are 404 so there is no TouristAttraction schema live. Google's knowledge graph uses TouristAttraction to associate tour operators with destinations. Perplexity and ChatGPT specifically look for Place and TouristAttraction to answer "what is the best tour for Ijen Crater" — JVTO cannot be cited in those answers without this schema.
Schema gap: no FAQPage schema on /travel-guide/faq despite FAQ content being present
The FAQ page has question/answer content in the DOM but zero FAQPage JSON-LD. FAQPage schema is the single highest-ROI schema type for AEO — it directly populates Google's "People Also Ask" boxes and is the primary source cited by AI engines for travel Q&A queries like "Is Ijen safe?", "What should I wear to Bromo?", "How do I book a private Bromo tour?"
Add FAQPage schema with at minimum 8–12 Q&A pairs. Use verbatim question phrasing from the SSOT FAQ section. Each answer should be 40–60 words — the ideal length for AI engine citation.
Tour pages use TouristTrip + Product but are missing key sub-properties
Live crawl confirms tour pages have TouristTrip and Product schema. However, key properties are absent: no itinerary (array of destinations), no touristType, no availabilityStarts, no offers.priceCurrency set to IDR. Bali departure pages also lack AggregateOffer on some packages. The Product schema uses Rupiah pricing but Google SGE prefers pricing in the user's currency — missing priceCurrency causes display errors.
Enrich TouristTrip with: itinerary: [{@type:"Place", name:"Mount Bromo"}, ...] from SSOT destination arrays. Add touristType: "Adventurous". Add priceCurrency:"IDR" and priceValidUntil to all Product/Offer nodes.
TravelAgency entity duplicated across pages with inconsistent data
Each tour page renders its own standalone TravelAgency block rather than referencing the homepage organisation entity via @id. The homepage version has sameAs array; individual tour pages omit it. Google's entity reconciliation treats these as separate entities, weakening Knowledge Panel signals.
Remove the full TravelAgency block from tour pages. Replace with a simple {"@type":"TravelAgency","@id":"https://javavolcano-touroperator.com/#organization"} reference node. The full entity lives only on the homepage.

No entity disambiguation for "Java" — high collision risk with Java programming language
AI engines frequently conflate "Java" (Indonesian island) with "Java" (programming language). None of the current page content includes explicit geographic disambiguation phrases. Queries like "Java volcano tour" may return programming content, and JVTO pages may not be cited in geographic Java queries if the entity is ambiguous. GEO requires entity anchoring.
Add geo-disambiguation anchors to every page's first paragraph: "East Java, Indonesia" instead of "Java". In schema, use areaServed: {"@type":"AdministrativeArea","name":"East Java, Indonesia"}. This is a GEO quick win.
Target market keywords (Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, Taiwan) absent from all page content
None of the 41 live pages contain explicit market-intent phrases such as "Bromo tour from Singapore", "East Java tour for Malaysian tourists", "private volcano tour from Hong Kong", or "台灣旅客火山旅遊". AI engines trained on search data associate travel products with departure markets — without these signals, JVTO will not appear in "best volcano tours from Singapore" queries on Perplexity, ChatGPT, or Google SGE.
Add departure-market landing sections or FAQ entries to /tours/from-surabaya and /tours/from-bali: "Travelling from Singapore/Malaysia/HK/Taiwan?" with routing info. This also creates anchor text for OTA deep links.
Proof / verification content (verify-jvto) not cross-linked from tour pages
The SSOT specifies a cross-link rule: every Travel Mode page must link to at least one Forensic Mode page. Live crawl of tour pages shows no in-content links to /verify-jvto or its sub-pages. AI engines use internal link graph to weight authority — trust pages that are not linked from transactional pages pass zero authority.
Add a "Why trust JVTO?" inline module on each tour page with a link to /verify-jvto. The SSOT cross-link rule already mandates this — it just needs deploying.
No structured content for Ijen blue fire visibility, Bromo weather windows, or seasonal closures
Travel AI engines frequently answer "when is the best time to see Ijen blue fire?" or "is Bromo open now?" — these are high-intent queries from JVTO's target markets. The SSOT contains this data (Bromo 5–15°C, Ijen 10–15°C, wind-dependent blue fire) but it is not on any crawlable page with proper heading structure. The /travel-guide/weather-and-closures page exists but its content depth is unknown.
Ensure weather/closure page has H2/H3 structured answers to: "best time for blue fire", "Bromo closure season", "Ijen opening hours". Add FAQPage schema. This is a direct AEO win.
