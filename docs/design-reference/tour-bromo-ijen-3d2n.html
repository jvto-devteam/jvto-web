Tour JVTO-01 · 3D · 2N

The single most-requested JVTO itinerary. It pairs the two iconic East Java sites in the most efficient order — Bromo sunrise on day two, Ijen blue fire on the night of day three. Private vehicle throughout; no mixed groups.

Day 1 · Surabaya → Cemoro Lawang

Airport / hotel pickup in Surabaya, drive to Bromo base village. Briefing and early dinner; sleep before midnight.

  • Pickup window: 12:00–15:00 from Juanda Airport or any Surabaya hotel
  • Stop at Probolinggo for lunch
  • Arrive Cemoro Lawang by 18:00
  • Itinerary briefing with lead guide
  • Lights out by 21:00

Day 2 · Bromo sunrise → Banyuwangi

Midnight wake-up, jeep to King Kong Hill for sunrise. Crater walk, then long transfer to Banyuwangi via the coastal road. Ijen clinic check in afternoon.

  • 00:30 wake-up, hot drink
  • 01:30 jeep transfer to King Kong Hill
  • 05:20 sunrise over Tengger caldera
  • 06:30 sea of sand + crater rim walk
  • 11:00 breakfast at Cemoro Lawang
  • 12:00 begin transfer to Banyuwangi (~6 h)
  • 17:00 Ijen clinic medical check (~15 min)
  • 19:00 dinner; lights out by 21:00

Day 3 · Ijen blue fire → return

01:00 ascent, blue fire at the rim, dawn over the acid lake, descent by morning. Transfer to your onward point — Surabaya, Bali ferry, or Banyuwangi station.

  • 00:30 wake-up, light breakfast
  • 01:00 transfer to Paltuding base camp
  • 01:30 ascent begins (3 km / 600 m vertical)
  • 03:00 blue fire at crater rim
  • 05:00 dawn over the acid lake
  • 07:30 descent to base
  • 09:00 transfer to Surabaya OR Bali ferry OR Banyuwangi station

Frequently asked.

About this route

  • Q1 Are your tours private or shared?

    All JVTO tours are private only. JVTO does not mix bookings with other guests or sell seat-in-coach options. Your vehicle, driver, and guide are arranged for your party alone.

  • Q2 What is included in the price?

    Private transport (AC MPV or Hiace by group size) plus a private 4WD jeep at Bromo, a dedicated crew, all entrance fees and permits, daily mineral water, full pick-up to drop-off assistance, a JVTO travel T-shirt, and meals as stated (breakfast minimum). As an Ijen route it also adds gas masks, trekking poles, and health-screening coordination where BBKSDA rules require it.

  • Q3 How does the Ijen health screening work?

    For tours that include the Ijen night hike, JVTO arranges health-screening coordination, conducted by licensed medical staff, as required under BBKSDA access regulations for the Ijen crater zone (Surat Edaran SE.1658/KSA.9/2024). Vitals recorded digitally include SpO2, blood pressure, heart rate, and respiratory history. The certificate carries a QR code verified by BBKSDA at the crater access gate — no valid QR code means no access to the crater zone.

  • Q4 Can you guarantee sunrise views or blue fire?

    No. Weather, cloud, volcanic gas, and other natural factors are outside JVTO's control. JVTO designs itineraries to maximise the chance of good conditions but cannot promise specific natural phenomena. Blue Fire is a natural phenomenon subject to weather and gas activity.

  • Q5 How do payments, deposits and deadlines work?

    Standard deposit is 20% of total booking value, paid by card via a secure JVTO link. If Day 1 is within 14 days, up to 100% may be required. Card balance is due no later than 5 days before Day 1; bank wire or Wise no later than 3 days before. Cash at the JVTO office only if approved in writing.

  • Q6 What happens if Bromo or Ijen is closed on my dates?

    JVTO follows official instructions from Indonesian authorities and park management. Depending on the situation, JVTO may adjust timings, use alternative viewpoints, replace destinations with safe alternatives, or activate its Plan B framework. Guests are briefed in advance and no guest is left without a program.
