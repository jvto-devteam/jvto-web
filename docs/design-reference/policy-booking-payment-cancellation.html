This policy applies to all enquiries, quotations, bookings, amendments, cancellations, and tour participation with Java Volcano Tour Operator (JVTO).

1 · Operator identity & official channels

Official channels — valid for booking, support, and payment verification:

  • Website & secure checkout: javavolcano-touroperator.com
  • WhatsApp: +62 822-4478-8833
  • Email: hello@javavolcano-touroperator.com
Official channels only

JVTO is not responsible for any offers, instructions, or payment requests made outside these official channels.

2 · Order of precedence

If there is any discrepancy, the following order applies:

  1. The specific terms on your Official E-Voucher / Invoice (PDF)
  2. This Booking, Payment & Cancellation Policy
  3. The Inclusions & Exclusions Policy
  4. The Privacy & Data Protection Policy
  5. The Travel Guide — Booking Information

3 · Nature of JVTO products

  • JVTO operates private, all-inclusive tours.
  • JVTO does not operate open join-in group tours and does not sell standalone "transport-only" services as its core product.
  • Only inclusions explicitly stated on the official tour page and/or your Official E-Voucher / Invoice (PDF) are contractually binding.

4 · When a booking is confirmed

A booking is confirmed only when all of the following are met:

  1. You select a specific official JVTO package; and
  2. Your required payment (deposit or full payment) is successfully processed via approved methods; and
  3. JVTO issues your Official E-Voucher / Invoice (PDF) confirming the booking details.

Enquiries, messages, and quotations are not binding until the booking is confirmed under the rule above. Official pricing and accounting are in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR).

5 · Payments

Deposit requirement

Standard deposit is 20% of the total booking value. If Day 1 is within 14 days, JVTO may require up to 100% full payment.

Approved methods & balance deadlines

Deposit: card payment via JVTO secure checkout. Balance: card (no later than 5 days before Day 1), bank transfer / Wise (no later than 3 days before Day 1), or cash at the JVTO office only if approved in writing.

Payment security & anti-fraud

Checkout is SSL-encrypted via a PCI DSS-compliant gateway. JVTO does not store full card numbers, CVV codes, or banking passwords — and will never ask for them via chat or email. If unsure, verify via WhatsApp or email before paying.

7 · Amendments & rescheduling

To correct booking details (name spelling, pickup, rooming), notify JVTO as soon as possible; some changes are subject to supplier rules, availability, and cost differences. Rescheduling requested 48 hours or more before Day 1 may be granted once without fee, subject to availability and seasonal/cost differences. Requests less than 48 hours before Day 1 are generally not permitted and may be treated as a late cancellation.

8 · Cancellation policy (48-hour cut-off)

Day 1 is the first day of services; the cut-off is measured in local Indonesia time.

48 hours or more before Day 1

Payments are not refundable in cash. 100% converts to JVTO Travel Credit — issued in IDR, non-expiring, transferable with written confirmation, and usable for future tours at prevailing rates.

Less than 48 hours before Day 1

The booking may be forfeited up to 100%. Travel Credit and cash refunds are generally not provided.

9 · Force majeure & external events

JVTO may partially or fully cancel, postpone, or reroute a tour due to events beyond reasonable control — volcanic activity, toxic gas, earthquakes, landslides, floods; government or park closures; strikes, civil unrest, security threats; major transport disruptions; or severe weather rendering services unsafe.

Where JVTO cancels or significantly alters services not due to guest breach, non-payment, or no-show, it may offer (considering irrecoverable third-party costs): reasonable alternatives of comparable value, Travel Credit covering the unused portion, or a partial/full cash refund net of non-recoverable expenses, as agreed in writing. Guest-initiated cancellations remain governed by Section 8.

10 · Health, fitness & eligibility

Guests are responsible for being medically and physically fit for the itinerary. Certain activities (volcano hikes, night trekking, sulfur gas exposure) may be unsuitable for specific conditions. Where health screening or permit conditions are imposed by authorities (e.g., Mount Ijen), guests must comply with the process arranged or recognised by JVTO; if a guest is denied clearance, affected components are non-refundable, as related costs are pre-committed. JVTO strongly recommends comprehensive travel insurance including medical and trip-interruption cover.

11 · Guest responsibilities

By confirming a booking, the lead guest and all participants agree to:

  • Provide accurate, complete information during enquiry and booking
  • Pay through official channels within deadlines
  • Review the Official E-Voucher promptly and report discrepancies
  • Submit pickup/logistical details within requested timeframes and be present on time
  • Follow safety instructions from JVTO crew and licensed guides
  • Respect local laws, cultures, communities, and environments
  • Hold appropriate personal travel insurance
