§ 01

What is ISIC?

UNESCO-endorsed

The International Student Identity Card (ISIC) is the only internationally recognised student identity document, endorsed by UNESCO and accepted in 130+ countries. It confirms full-time student status for enrolled students — card validity is confirmed via the Alive Verify API at the time of booking.

ISIC verifies provider eligibility through its Alive Verify API — a real-time authentication check. JVTO is a registered provider in the ISIC network. If you are not yet a cardholder, cards are issued through national student unions and ISIC offices worldwide.

How verification works.

Live & automated

  1. Share your card number
