§ 01

Why start from Surabaya?

Hub overview

Every JVTO tour from Surabaya is 100% private — your group, your vehicle, your schedule. Mr. Sam, the founder, is an active officer of the Indonesian Tourist Police (Ditpamobvit East Java): every route, safety decision, and written rule traces back to someone who answers to police protocol.

What's included is written before you book. What it costs is confirmed before you pay. Surabaya's Juanda Airport is the natural entry point for East Java — Bromo is reachable in around 4 hours via Probolinggo, and Ijen via the Surabaya–Banyuwangi corridor.
