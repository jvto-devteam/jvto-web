This policy defines what is included and not included in JVTO private tour packages. Only inclusions written on your specific Official E-Voucher / Invoice (PDF) are contractually binding.

The write-it-to-bind-it rule

Only inclusions that appear on the official JVTO package page for your chosen tour and/or your Official E-Voucher / Invoice (PDF) are legally binding components of your package.

If it is not written, it is not included

If it is not in your confirmed inclusions and voucher, it is not part of the contracted package — even if discussed informally.

What is included

Transportation

Private air-conditioned vehicle for all land sectors listed in your itinerary, with fuel, tolls, and standard parking. Vehicle allocation by group size:

Group sizeVehicle
2–3 guests1 × MPV (e.g., Toyota Avanza or similar)
4–9 guests1 × Toyota Hiace (or similar 16-seat minibus)
10–11 guests1 × Toyota Hiace + 1 × MPV (seating & luggage)

When Bromo is included: private 4WD jeep(s) for sunrise and crater viewpoints — max ±4 guests per jeep for comfort and safety, with additional jeeps for larger groups.

Accommodation

Hotel / homestay for all overnight stays in your itinerary. Standard allocation is 1 room per 2 guests (King or Twin); odd-numbered groups get an extra bed in one room. Maximum 2 guests per standard room. Room upgrades may be available at a published supplement, subject to request and availability.

Professional crew

Group sizeCrew
2–3 guests1 × driver-guide (English-speaking) + licensed local site guides where required
4–11 guests1 × professional driver + 1 × escort guide + licensed local site guides at key locations
10–11 guestsAdditional driver for the second vehicle; escort guide travels with the main group

Crew duties: safe driving, time management, coordination with hotels/jeeps/ferries/park authorities, and basic route explanations and guest support.

Tickets, permits & local logistics

All listed entrance tickets and park permits for your confirmed itinerary, where applicable — Mount Bromo National Park access, Mount Ijen (including crater access where legally permitted), Madakaripura, Tumpak Sewu, other named attractions, and local fees explicitly built into the package.

Mount Ijen safety & trekking support (when Ijen is included)

Professional gas mask (rated for sulfur) for each participating guest
Trekking poles (shared or individual, depending on configuration)
Ijen medical check & clearance arranged by JVTO, by licensed medical personnel at an approved facility or at your Bondowoso-area hotel (included for Ijen itineraries)

Meals & water

Daily bottled mineral water in the vehicle for overland sectors, and hotel breakfasts for each included overnight stay. Additional meals are included only when explicitly stated on the package page and/or your voucher.

Ferry, branded inclusions & on-trip support

Java–Bali ferry tickets where listed and specified as included; 1 × JVTO travel T-shirt per participant on eligible packages (custom group T-shirts for groups of ≥12 where written); pre-trip assistance via official WhatsApp/email; clear pickup instructions and emergency contacts on your voucher; on-trip coordination for timing, weather/closure adjustments, and incident first response.

Conditional inclusions (only if written)

The following are included only when clearly stated on both the official package description and your Official E-Voucher / Invoice (PDF):

  • Custom JVTO group T-shirts (design, quantity, eligibility)
  • Additional hosted meals — e.g., dinner in Bondowoso before the Ijen night trek; lunch after returning from Ijen; lunch near Yanto Homestay after Tumpak Sewu; a farewell dinner
  • Bali hotel/airport transfers beyond the standard ferry crossing
  • Early check-in / late check-out at hotels
  • Dedicated photographer / videographer services
  • Police escort or special convoy arrangements for VIP/large groups (subject to availability and regulation)

What is not included

To keep things clear: anything not explicitly listed as an inclusion on the official package page and on your Official E-Voucher / Invoice (PDF) is excluded. Common examples (unless clearly written as included):

  • International / domestic flights and entry visas
  • Travel insurance of any kind
  • Meals not specified (including alcohol)
  • Tips / gratuities for guides and drivers
  • Personal expenses (snacks, souvenirs, laundry)
