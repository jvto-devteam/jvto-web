Destination overview

One of the most recognisable landscapes on earth.

Mount Bromo sits inside the vast Tengger Caldera — a ~10 km² expanse of grey volcanic sand that gives the "Sea of Sand" its name. Bromo's crater vents a near-constant smoke plume while the higher caldera rim frames the horizon.

The draw is three-layered: the Penanjakan viewpoint at 2,770 m for the pre-dawn panorama; the 4WD jeep traverse across the sea of sand — the experience itself, not just access; and the crater approach to an actively smoking vent. Most come for the sunrise. Many remember the crater walk.

§ 02

Volcanic status notice.

Verify before travel
Level II — Waspada (Elevated), effective 2026-05-16

PVMBG raised Mount Bromo to Level II Waspada on 16 May 2026, establishing a 1 km exclusion zone around the crater rim. Verify the current PVMBG/BBKSDA status before you travel — this notice reflects a one-time reading and no live update feed exists.

Fully accessible

The core experience is unaffected.

The Penanjakan sunrise viewpoint (2,770 m), the sea-of-sand jeep traverse, and the Pura Luhur Poten temple area on the caldera floor all sit outside the 1 km radius and remain open.

Currently restricted

The crater lip only.

The final approach to the crater rim — directly over the active vent — falls within the exclusion zone. JVTO will not take guests to the crater lip while Level II is in effect.

Effect on your itinerary · minimal

§ 05

Practical information.

Plan your day

Best season

Dry season, April–October — clear skies and firm volcanic sand. Wet season brings mist and muddy access roads; sunrise views may be obstructed.

Temperature at Penanjakan (pre-dawn)

5–15°C. Warm layers are required — wind chill at the viewpoint is significant regardless of the daytime temperature lower down.

Physical requirements

The Penanjakan viewpoint and sea-of-sand jeep portion have no significant physical demands beyond normal mobility. The crater approach (currently restricted under Level II) involves a short walk or optional horse ride on uneven terrain.
