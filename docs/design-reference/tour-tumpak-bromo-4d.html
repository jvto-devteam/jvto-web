Tour JVTO-03 · 4D · 3N

For travelers who want depth over speed. The 4-day pace lets you photograph Tumpak Sewu at multiple light angles, recover before Bromo, and add the lesser-known Madakaripura on the return leg.

Day 1 · Surabaya → Tumpak Sewu

Pickup in Surabaya, drive south to Lumajang, afternoon at Tumpak Sewu viewpoint and canyon descent.

  • 07:00 Surabaya pickup
  • 12:30 arrive Lumajang
  • 13:30 panorama viewpoint
  • 14:30 canyon descent (~90 min)
  • 17:00 return to viewpoint, sunset
  • Overnight in local homestay

Day 2 · Goa Tetes → Bromo

Morning at Goa Tetes cave waterfalls. Afternoon transfer to Cemoro Lawang.

  • 08:00 Goa Tetes hike
  • 12:00 lunch in Lumajang
  • 13:00 transfer to Cemoro Lawang (~4 h)
  • 17:00 arrival, briefing
  • 21:00 lights out

Day 3 · Bromo sunrise + Madakaripura

Classic Bromo sunrise sequence in the morning. Madakaripura waterfall in the afternoon.

  • 00:30 wake-up, jeep transfer
  • 05:20 sunrise at King Kong Hill
  • 06:30 sea of sand + crater walk
  • 11:00 breakfast
  • 13:00 transfer to Madakaripura
  • 14:30 hike through the ravine
  • 17:00 return to base, dinner

Day 4 · Return to Surabaya

Slow morning, then transfer to Surabaya airport or city hotel.

  • 08:00 breakfast
  • 09:00 transfer
  • 14:00 Surabaya airport drop

The canyon descent is optional

Tumpak Sewu has two ways to experience it: the rim viewpoint (a short walk from parking, suitable for all fitness levels) and the base canyon descent (around 300 steps with ropes in places, moderate-to-challenging). Your guide assesses fitness on the day and briefs the descent before starting — you can stay at the rim if you prefer.

Bring a raincoat for Madakaripura

The approach to Madakaripura runs through a narrow canyon with falling water — you will get wet. A raincoat is strongly recommended; helmets are handed out by the local site management at the entrance.

§ 05

Frequently asked.

About this route

  • Q1 Are your tours private or shared?

    All JVTO tours are private only. JVTO does not mix bookings with other guests or sell seat-in-coach options. Your vehicle, driver, and guide are arranged for your party alone.

  • Q2 What is included in the price?

    Private AC transport with an English-speaking driver-guide plus a private 4WD jeep at Bromo, all entrance fees and permits (including Tumpak Sewu and Madakaripura), three overnight stays, daily mineral water, hotel breakfasts, a JVTO travel T-shirt, and 24/7 operations support. Madakaripura helmets are provided by local site management, not by JVTO.

  • Q3 How fit do I need to be for the Tumpak Sewu canyon?

    The rim viewpoint suits all fitness levels with a short walk from parking. The base canyon descent is around 300 steps with ropes in places and needs moderate-to-challenging fitness. JVTO guides assess fitness on the day and brief the descent before starting; you can stay at the rim if you prefer.

  • Q4 Can you guarantee sunrise views?

    No. Weather, cloud, and other natural factors are outside JVTO's control. JVTO designs the itinerary to maximise the chance of good conditions but cannot promise specific natural phenomena.

  • Q5 What should I wear and bring?

    Layered clothing for the cold pre-dawn Bromo start, closed shoes with good grip for the canyon, and a raincoat — you will get wet at Madakaripura. Sunscreen and sunglasses for daytime stops.

  • Q6 How do payments, deposits and deadlines work?

    Standard deposit is 20% of total booking value, paid by card via a secure JVTO link. If Day 1 is within 14 days, up to 100% may be required. Card balance is due no later than 5 days before Day 1; bank wire or Wise no later than 3 days before. Cash at the JVTO office only if approved in writing.
