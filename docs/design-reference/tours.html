§ 05

Planning your expedition.

Three decision factors

Consider your time

Multi-day tours provide a deeper experience; day trips are optimized for tight schedules with a Surabaya turn-around.

Consider your fitness

Ijen and Tumpak Sewu require moderate physical effort; Bromo is accessible to most fitness levels.

Consider your origin

Surabaya is the most efficient hub for Bromo; Bali is a popular starting point for Ijen-first itineraries.
