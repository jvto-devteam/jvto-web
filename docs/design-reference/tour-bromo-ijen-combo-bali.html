§ 05

Frequently asked.

About this route

  • Q1 How does the Ijen health screening work?

    For tours that include the Ijen night hike, JVTO arranges health-screening coordination, conducted by licensed medical staff, as required under BBKSDA access regulations for the Ijen crater zone (Surat Edaran SE.1658/KSA.9/2024). Vitals recorded digitally include SpO2, blood pressure, heart rate, and respiratory history. The certificate carries a QR code verified by BBKSDA at the crater access gate — no valid QR code means no access to the crater zone.

  • Q2 Is the Java–Bali ferry included?

    Yes. For Bali-origin packages the Gilimanuk–Ketapang ferry crossings are included in the base price on both the outbound and return legs, as listed on your voucher.

  • Q3 Can you guarantee sunrise views or blue fire?

    No. Weather, cloud, volcanic gas, and other natural factors are outside JVTO's control. JVTO designs itineraries to maximise the chance of good conditions but cannot promise specific natural phenomena. Blue Fire is a natural phenomenon subject to weather and gas activity.

  • Q4 Are your tours private or shared?

    All JVTO tours are private only. JVTO does not mix bookings with other guests or sell seat-in-coach options. Your vehicle, driver, and guide are arranged for your party alone.

  • Q5 How do payments, deposits and deadlines work?

    Standard deposit is 20% of total booking value, paid by card via a secure JVTO link. If Day 1 is within 14 days, up to 100% may be required. Card balance is due no later than 5 days before Day 1; bank wire or Wise no later than 3 days before. Cash at the JVTO office only if approved in writing.

  • Q6 What happens if Bromo or Ijen is closed on my dates?

    JVTO follows official instructions from Indonesian authorities and park management. Depending on the situation, JVTO may adjust timings, use alternative viewpoints, replace destinations with safe alternatives, or activate its Plan B framework. Guests are briefed in advance and no guest is left without a program.
