A · Booking, contact & logistics

JVTO's official channels are WhatsApp (+62 822 4478 8833, primary) and email (hello@javavolcano-touroperator.com). Both appear on your E-Voucher and confirmation. WhatsApp is the fastest channel for real-time logistics.

Two official paths. Website Instant Book — choose your package, date and travelers, review price and add-ons, accept the Terms & Cancellation Policy, and pay. WhatsApp-assisted — send your dates, origin, group size and requirements; JVTO confirms availability and price, sends a secure payment link, and issues your E-Voucher. A 20% deposit confirms the booking either way.

Standard deposit is 20% of total booking value, paid by card via a secure JVTO link. If Day 1 is within 14 days, up to 100% may be required. Card balance is due no later than 5 days before Day 1; bank wire or Wise no later than 3 days before. Cash at the JVTO office only if approved in writing.

The full booking and cancellation text is published at /policy/booking-payment-cancellation.

B · What’s included & tour format

All JVTO tours are private only. JVTO does not mix bookings with other guests or sell seat-in-coach options. Your vehicle, driver, and guide are arranged for your party alone.

All packages include private transport (AC MPV or Hiace by group size), a dedicated crew, all entrance fees and permits, daily mineral water, full pick-up to drop-off assistance, a JVTO travel T-shirt, and meals as stated (breakfast minimum). Bromo-inclusive packages add a private 4WD jeep. Ijen-relevant packages add gas masks, trekking poles, and health-screening coordination where BBKSDA rules require it. Bali-origin packages add the Gilimanuk–Ketapang ferry. Madakaripura helmets are provided by local site management — not by JVTO. See the written inclusions & exclusions.

Yes. JVTO is an official ISIC provider (ID 259268, verifiable at isic.org). Verified international students access adapted rates on selected packages via the ISIC Alive Verify API.

One main suitcase or backpack per person plus one small daypack. For multi-destination routes with hotel changes, packing light is recommended.

Layered clothing for cold pre-dawn starts; closed shoes with good grip (hiking shoes recommended for Ijen and Tumpak Sewu); a raincoat (essential for Madakaripura, where you will get wet in the canyon); sunscreen and sunglasses for daytime stops. JVTO provides headlamps on Ijen routes.

C · Ijen health screening

For tours that include the Ijen night hike, JVTO arranges health-screening coordination — conducted by licensed medical staff — as required under BBKSDA access regulations for the crater zone. Vitals recorded digitally include SpO₂, blood pressure, heart rate, and respiratory history. The certificate carries a QR code verified by BBKSDA at the access gate. No valid QR code means no access to the crater zone. Full protocol on the Ijen health-screening guide.Conditional · BBKSDA Surat Edaran SE.1658/KSA.9/2024

If screening staff or the JVTO team advise that you should not attempt the hike, JVTO follows that decision. The climb is cancelled for that individual, not the group; you can wait at base camp or a safe zone while the group continues. The screening session cost is non-refundable.This is a BBKSDA safety rule, not JVTO discretion

D · During the tour

Many families and older travellers have joined JVTO tours safely. The key is matching itinerary difficulty to real fitness and health. Some routes — the Tumpak Sewu canyon descent, the Ijen night hike — demand moderate fitness. JVTO advises on route selection during the inquiry stage.

E · Closures, weather & Plan B

JVTO follows official instructions from Indonesian authorities and park management. Depending on the situation, JVTO may adjust timings, use alternative viewpoints, replace destinations with safe alternatives, or activate its Plan B framework. Guests are briefed in advance and no guest is left without a program.

Light or normal rain rarely cancels an entire tour, but it can make trails slippery, reduce visibility, or require timing and route changes. For safety-critical sections such as the Ijen crater rim and the Madakaripura canyon, JVTO may delay or reroute. Sunrise and Blue Fire views are conditional on weather.

F · Cancellation & Travel Credit

JVTO uses a Travel Credit system instead of cash refunds for guest-initiated cancellations. Cancel at least 48 hours before Day 1 and 100% of eligible booking value converts to Lifetime Travel Credit (no expiry, transferable). Cancel within 48 hours of Day 1 and payment is fully forfeited with no Travel Credit. JVTO offers one free reschedule if requested at least 48 hours before Day 1.

G · Police escort & group benefits

For large groups (typically around 18 guests or more), JVTO can coordinate an official traffic police escort (Patwal) on certain road segments — for example from a toll exit to Bondowoso — when approved by the relevant Traffic Police unit. This is a coordination service, not a default inclusion. See Police escort for groups.
